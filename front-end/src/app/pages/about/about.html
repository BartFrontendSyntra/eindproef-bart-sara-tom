About Forestmaster

This application is designed to support the efficient management and preservation of forest areas by improving communication between visitors, rangers, and administrators.

Users can easily report issues they encounter in the forest, such as fallen trees, damaged paths, illegal dumping, or safety concerns. In addition, they can mark points of interest, including scenic locations, wildlife sightings, or notable natural features. These contributions help build a richer, real-time overview of the forest environment.

Rangers have access to a centralized system where all reports and points of interest are collected. They can review submissions in a structured list or visualize them on an interactive map. This allows for quicker prioritization, better planning of maintenance activities, and more effective responses to urgent situations.

By combining community input with professional oversight, the application helps maintain safer, cleaner, and more enjoyable forest spaces for everyone.
